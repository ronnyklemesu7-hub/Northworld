About Northwold

An Oasis of Tranquility

Tucked away from the vibrant energy of Accra, Northwold Hotel stands as a premier destination for travelers seeking a peaceful retreat without compromising on modern convenience. Located in the serene neighborhood of Sotuom, our hotel is meticulously designed to be more than just a place to sleep—it is a destination for rejuvenation.

From the minimalist elegance of our architecture to the lush surroundings that invite calm, we cater to the discerning guest. Whether for a high-powered business trip, a romantic getaway, or an unforgettable event, our team ensures every moment is defined by comfort.
